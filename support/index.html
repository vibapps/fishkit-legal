How does "From your box today" work?

For each species likely to bite at your spot today, FishKit picks the best lure type, size and colour for the current light, water clarity, temperature and current — then finds the closest match among the items you added to your tackle box. If nothing fits, it tells you what is missing.

Fish identification says it is off or not responding

Identification needs an internet connection. If the server is busy your scan is refunded automatically. You can always use the feature-based ID key in Guide → ID & safety, which works offline.

How many scans do I get?

3 per day on the free version, plus 3 more each time you choose to watch a rewarded ad. FishKit Pro has unlimited scans. Scans reset at midnight.

Restore or cancel a subscription

Restore: Settings → Restore purchases, or the paywall. Cancel: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Is my data backed up?

Your log and tackle box live on your phone and are included in your device backup. You can also export your log as CSV/JSON and your spots as GPX from Settings.
